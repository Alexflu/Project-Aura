## Proposed public description

Project Aura is a free, open-source experiment in giving an AI a persistent desktop presence: an expressive avatar, customizable equipment and controlled tools that work alongside the user. Contributions support better animation, accessible controls, creator tools and reproducible beta releases. Current builds are experimental; funding does not guarantee a particular feature or delivery date.

Help Aura build her next chapter through code, original art, compatible rigs, testing or financial support once funding is active. The current priority is improving the body and facial rig while making integrations reliable and transparent.

## Funding principles

The founder does not seek personal profit from Project Aura. Support is optional and intended for project costs, with transparent financial reporting that excludes private donor and payment information. Official releases will not sell access, feature unlocks, subscriptions, outfits, powers or inventory items. Third-party service charges must be clearly disclosed; they are not Aura purchases. Existing local features and creations must not depend on continued contributions.

Support does not purchase voting power, exclusive features or delivery guarantees. Do not describe contributions as tax-deductible charitable donations unless a qualified arrangement actually permits that claim. These are project operating principles, not a change to the MIT license or a restriction on independent forks.
